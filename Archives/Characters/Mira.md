##### **II. DISCOVERY**
Mira came to CAIN’s attention approximately 3 years prior, following an anomalous homicide during a private art gallery exhibition in ████████.
The event coincided with a localized blackout and a surge of psychic resonance consistent with TOAD-class manifestations. The anomaly—later confirmed as a weak CAT 1 TOAD—had emerged from Mira’s suppressed resentment and the desire to reclaim recognition for stolen artistic work.

The TOAD killed her employer, a renowned sculptor who had publicly taken credit for years of her work. Local authorities investigating the scene reported “temperature distortion,” “melting marble,” and “unseen force vibrations.”
CAIN Executioners, deployed under the cover of detectives, intercepted Mira within a nearby storage facility. The ensuing engagement lasted several hours and resulted in multiple personnel developing short-term sickness from residual psychic afterburn.

Mira’s Anomaly was neutralized during containment; she was subsequently sedated and transported under authorization of ████████████ to [[Watchsite-12]] for rehabilitation and observation.

**Containment Note**: Prolonged activation (>7 minutes) induces rapid muscular fatigue, dehydration, and risk of dermal burns from internal thermal rebound.

##### **IV. BACKGROUND (RESTRICTED)**

Before containment, Mira worked as a personal assistant to acclaimed sculptor █████ ███████. Though publicly regarded as his aide, internal records and witness accounts indicate that Mira was the true artist behind a majority of his celebrated works.
Repeated psychological manipulation, gaslighting, and financial exploitation over several years culminated in the emotional fracturing that birthed her TOAD manifestation.

CAIN’s post-incident evaluation determined that Mira was under substantial psychic duress during the fatal event. She expressed no recollection of her Anomaly’s actions, only a persistent “heat” and “weight” in her hands.

Following neutralization and transfer, Mira was designated for long-term containment therapy at Watchsite-12. Her recovery has since shown steady progress under the supervision of Dr. E. Hart.

##### **V. CURRENT STATUS**
Mira currently serves as an Active Field Agent stationed at Watchsite-12.
She demonstrates strong proficiency in FORCE and CONDITIONING fields, with tactical aptitude in direct engagement and temperature-based containment operations. However, she remains deficient in COVERT, INVESTIGATION, and SURVEILLANCE disciplines.
Dr. Hart continues to oversee her behavioral evaluation, citing “measured improvement” but cautioning against field pairing with unstable or high-emotion operatives.
Mira’s demeanor is consistently professional, though interpersonal analyses note mild detachment and underlying guilt concerning her former occupation.
**Recommendation**: Maintain structured deployment environments with clear objectives. Avoid extended psychological probing or forced recollection of pre-containment trauma.
