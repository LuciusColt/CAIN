## **SECTION I: TRANSFER**

> *He had been warned once, in jest, that Watchsite-44 was a place where angels were sent to learn cruelty.*
> *He thought that was an exaggeration.*
> *It wasn’t.*

Lucian’s transfer from Watchsite-12 occurred at 0600 hours, following a direct order from Oversight. He was sent to collect two Executioners for a joint operation under the jurisdiction of Watchsite-44 command.

The facility itself appeared governmental at a glance — marble facade, mirrored glass, the smell of polished sterility — but beneath the surface sprawled an underground complex of concrete caverns, reinforced gates, and humming generators.

Discipline, as it turned out, was non-existent.

Executioners in Watchsite-44 did not train — they hunted one another.
The strong survived. The weak fed the strong. The Blackcoats watched and took notes.

Upon arrival, Lucian was intercepted by Blackcoats who — in a **clerical “error”** — threw him into *the Pit*, a massive sublevel arena where the Executioners engaged in constant combat “to hone skill.”

For twenty minutes, Lucian fought for his life.
For twenty minutes, *no one stopped him.*

He broke one jaw, dislocated his own shoulder, and split open his lip before a siren cut through the chaos and the field above crackled with electricity. The air shimmered. The crowd dispersed.

He was dragged from the blood-slick concrete by two guards in masks and brought before **Director Harrow**, the commanding officer of Watchsite-44.

> “A mix-up,” said Harrow, eyes unmoved. “You weren’t meant to be thrown in. But I won’t apologize — you held your own. That’s what matters here.”

Two Executioners waited behind the Director’s desk, untouched by the chaos.
These would be Lucian’s temporary partners.

> **Presumed Manifestation Type:** HOUND.

**NOTES:**
The anomaly originated after officers apprehended a known serial offender exhibiting extreme psychosis.
Subject underwent rapid transformation upon detention — body structure destabilized, cranial mass split open, elongated.
The resulting creature slaughtered twenty-seven officers and overran the building.

**Mission Priority:** Eliminate anomaly. Civilian status non-essential.

## **SECTION IV: DEPLOYMENT**

Insertion was conducted via rotary air transport.
Landing: rooftop of PD-77 at 21:14 hours.
Weather: static haze, heavy particulate rain.
Visibility: 15 meters.

The team descended through a roof access stairwell.
The walls were **wet**. Not with water. Something else.

Inside, the lights flickered intermittently.
The scent was ammonia, metal, and meat.
Floor by floor, the corridors pulsed with faint movement — a living structure adapting to intrusion.

---

## **SECTION V: CONTACT — “TRACES”**

> *Faceless.*
> *Headless.*
> *Still wearing uniforms.*

Traces were encountered on the second floor — humanoid remnants that fired blindly in response to sound.
Their bodies resembled mannequins wearing police uniforms, their skin smooth and uninterrupted where the head should be.

> Maeve whispered: “They’re not people.”
> Lucian whispered back: “They were something’s idea of one.”

They moved in silence, breathing through the rhythm of the flickering lights.
Every noise — a step, a cough, a reload — brought gunfire.
Lucian silenced three of them with surgical precision, cutting their trigger fingers before they could fire.

---

## **SECTION VI: INITIAL ENCOUNTER**

Timestamp: 23:09

The team entered the holding cells and came face to face with the **Hound.**
The creature appeared as a swollen, four-limbed distortion of human anatomy, its torso hollow, ribs outward like broken cage bars.
Its head — or what remained of one — was split vertically. It screamed through both halves.

Lucian’s Sparrow-25 emptied an entire magazine before the thing flinched.
Maeve deployed a resonance charge.
Orrin provided suppressing fire until his rifle jammed.

The Hound retreated into the lower levels.
The team locked the basement access with three steel bars and an iron desk, sealing the creature below.

---

## **SECTION VII: INTERLUDE**

They barricaded the precinct chief’s office for the night.

Orrin’s hands shook when he cleaned his weapon.
Maeve checked Lucian’s arm — fracture confirmed, reset with a belt and swearing.

Outside, lightning illuminated the old city through broken glass.

They ate compressed nutrition bars.
Lucian tasted copper and silence.

> *“You ever notice how the lights flicker before something bad happens?” he asked.*
> Maeve smiled faintly. “Maybe that’s the world blinking.”
> Orrin: “Then maybe we should blink back.”

The wind howled like sirens.

---

## **SECTION VIII: SECOND DESCENT**

The next morning, they descended into the basement.
The air was humid, foul, resonating with low, almost-human whispers.
The floor was painted in circular streaks — not ritual, just instinct.
Something *learning to mark its territory.*

They passed a partially melted evidence locker. Inside: mannequins fused together in a wall of pink wax and bone.
Lucian said nothing.

---

## **SECTION IX: “SAWTHING”**

Designation: Trace Evolution
Height: 2.4 meters
Primary Weapon: Circular saw blade fused into right forearm
Behavior: Slow, uncoordinated, but relentless

The battle lasted **two hours and thirty-six minutes.**
Orrin lost his footing twice. Maeve detonated two charges.
Lucian severed the creature’s spinal column using his Scissors.
The body fell and kept twitching for six minutes after death.

In the silence that followed, no one spoke.

---

## **SECTION X: FINAL ENCOUNTER — “THE HOUND”**

Location: Basement corridor, sublevel three.
Visibility: zero.
Heat signature: erratic.
Acoustic feedback: 140 dB at peak.

Lucian led the charge.
Maeve primed three flash grenades.
Orrin carried the flammable gel.

They engaged in total darkness.

The Hound was waiting.
It struck first — tearing the door frame in half, dragging Maeve into the dark.
Lucian dove after her, cutting blindly, the smell of burnt flesh and ozone thick enough to choke on.
Flash. Light. Heat.
The gel ignited.

In those seconds of light, all three struck together — Scissors, blade, and flame.
The Hound screamed until its voice folded in on itself and vanished.

Then silence.
No pulse.
No more movement.

---

## **SECTION XI: EXTRACTION**

They surfaced at dawn.
Air transport arrived thirty-seven minutes later.

Lucian remained silent the entire flight back.
Maeve slept against the window.
Orrin cleaned his weapon until the metal shone through the grime.
