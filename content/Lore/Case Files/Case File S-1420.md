##### **I. OVERVIEW**
At approximately 21:47 hours, local authorities responded to reports of mass hysteria and structural damage within the ████████ University Art and Humanities Building.
Witnesses described a “laughter-like distortion” and “reflections moving independently.” Local responders were incapacitated by acute psychological interference consistent with Anomalous activity, recorded at being Category 4.

CAIN Executioner Unit-12 was deployed from Watchsite-08 at 22:13 hours. Upon arrival, the structure was in partial collapse, with multiple casualties and signs of sustained reality displacement.

##### **II. CASUALTY REPORT**
**Civilian Fatalities**: 1 (student — Kayla ██████)  
**Civilians Injured**: 7 (moderate to severe psychological trauma)  
**CAIN Casualties**: 0  
**Recovered Subjects**: 1 (designated “Host Candidate L”)  
> This mission was deemed highly successful by CAIN overseers. Executioners we're reprimanded for failing to Wipe away the Stain in its entirety.

Victim Kayla ██████ was discovered at the epicenter of the event, exhibiting cranial and thoracic lacerations consistent with mirrored fragmentation wounds. Subject pronounced deceased at 22:28.

##### **III. OBSERVATIONS**
Upon containment, Host Candidate L was discovered in a state of shock, kneeling beside the victim and surrounded by a reflective distortion field. Object of interest (a child’s safety scissors, cobalt-handled) was found within his immediate possession.

Initial attempts to remove the object resulted in neurological instability and psychic energy discharge, leading to temporary containment under emergency protocol C-12: Partial Host Possession.
>See Equipment Dossier — [[Artifact 12-C]] for further information.

Subsequent testing confirmed the CAT 4 Anomaly entity (codename ████████) had transferred partial residual energy into Host Candidate L, producing a CAT 1 object-bound artifact.

##### **IV. SITE ANALYSIS**
Post-incident investigation revealed significant anomalous signatures within a 30-meter radius:
- Electromagnetic irregularities consistent with psychic phenomena.
- Visual anomalies within reflective surfaces; reflections delayed by 3–5 seconds.
- Auditory phenomena: repeated laughter, later traced to acoustic mirroring of nearby frequencies.
- Structural damage consistent with implosion from within.

All reflective materials on site were collected and transferred to CAIN Watchsite-12, Storage Wing E pending decontamination.

##### **V. CONTAINMENT & AFTER-ACTION SUMMARY**
Host Candidate L was placed under sedation and transported to CAIN Watchsite-12 for quarantine and observation.

Following three weeks of monitoring, psychic energy output stabilized to CAT 1 levels, confirming the host’s survivable compatibility with the residual entity.

Decision rendered by Oversight Command to retain Host Candidate L under supervision and offer integration into CAIN Operations Division, contingent on continuous psychological evaluation and suppression measures.

##### **VI. RECOMMENDATIONS**
- Implement reflective-surface screening during all campus surveillance operations.
- Classify incident to local Police, Government, and Associate Press under “Small gas leak explosion with slight structural damage.”
- Continue observation of Host Candidate L for signs of Anomaly reformation.
