**RESONANCE**  
*Passive: At the start of the mission, roll on the resonance table. When you make this roll, 1d3, then 1d6, then check the resonance tables listed below. When you are making an action roll and you are using an item you are resonant with, you gain a +1D bonus. You can spend a burst any time to roll an additional resonance. You can keep up to three at a time, and only benefit from one at a time.*
