---
title: "[ BEAST ]"
---
Objective:  
► Get into a fight  
► **Hold Back**  

Abilities:  
► **Insects!!!**: You have +1D when inflicting harm or violence against human opponents.  
► **I’ll take you down with me**: Regain 1d3 burst when you take an injury, fill out a hook, or take affliction.  
► **Rule of Nature**: When you inflict violence with an action, if you roll two or more 6s, the action inflicts 1 more slash on any tag and you regain 1 burst.  
► **Red Muscle**: You can take 2 nonlethal damage to gain +1D on any violent or forceful roll.  
► **Bare Teeth**: While you have two or more injuries or afflictions, it is not longer hard to use mundane capabilities against supernatural forces and your human physical abilities are at 1/2 CAT instead of CAT 0.
