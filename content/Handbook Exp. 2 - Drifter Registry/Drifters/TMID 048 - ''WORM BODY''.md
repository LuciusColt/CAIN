## WORM BODY
**Anomaly**

Could be mistaken for a tall person at a distance. Appearances only observed in neglected properties, especially residential. Entire entity appears to be made up of writhing, splitting worms.

**EXECUTION**:  
3 (solo), 6 (group), 10 (large group) 

**STRESS INFLICTED**:  
(1) 2 stress and worm hook (2/3): 1 stress and worm hook, (4+): 1 stress. 

**BEHAVIOR**:  
Attracted to breathing creatures, following exhalations. Limited other senses.

**SPECIALS**:  
- **Worm** - **Hook**. Inflict with a threat or when inflicting stress. A worm has burrowed into your body. An executioner recovers -1 stress from any resting die for each tick on the hook. If the hook fills up, roll 1d6. (1-3): suffer instant death (may resist as normal), or (4-6): Clear all slashes on hook and infect another executioner with this hook. If no other executioners are uninfected, may infect an NPC instead. Otherwise, suffer instant death.
