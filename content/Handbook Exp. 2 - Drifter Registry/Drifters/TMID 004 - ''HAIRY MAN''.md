## HAIRY MAN
**Anomaly**

Appears in mostly in woodland or rural settings. “Hair” is thick and profusive. Humanlike digitation. Has no digestive system and regurgitates the hair and bones of prey, uses pharyngeal jaw to “eat”. Capable of mimicking human speech.

**EXECUTION**:  
4 (solo), 8 (pair)  

**STRESS INFLICTED** (as reaction):  
(1) 4 stress, (2/3): 3 stress, (4+): 2 stress.  

**BEHAVIOR**:  
Follows at a distance, prefers to pick off lone prey. Hunts, mutilates, and masticates wildlife or livestock and regurgitates the remnants.  

**SPECIAL**:  
- **Ambush** - As a **tension move**, the Admin may inform the executioners they are being stalked. If nothing is done to mitigate this, any time they get into a conflict scene for the rest of the mission there’s a 50% chance (roll 1d6) a Hairy Man will attack and try to drag one of them off.
