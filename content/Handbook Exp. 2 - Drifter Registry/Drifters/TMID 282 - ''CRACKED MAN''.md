## CRACKED MAN
**Anomaly**

Found most often in abandoned places that had a lot of children - homes, schools, childcare centers.

**EXECUTION**:  
4 (solo), 8 (small group) 

**STRESS INFLICTED**:  
(1) 5 stress, (2/3): 4 stress, (4+): 3 stress.  

**BEHAVIOR**:  
Violent and extraordinarily belligerent once found. When it encounters doors or windows, it spends a long time figuring out how to open them.  

**SPECIAL**:  
- **Split** - **Threat** (1-2): Body part starts to splits off, weeping ichor. Becomes able to act with the body part at a distance, increasing execution talisman by +2 if threat is executed upon.
