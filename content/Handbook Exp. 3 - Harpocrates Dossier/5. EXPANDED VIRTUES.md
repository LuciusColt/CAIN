## EXPANDED VIRTUES

Certain executioners whose categories have reached CAT 6 or above are deemed **virtues**, individuals who time and time again have survived in instances where their comrades and allies have been slain. Their power verges on inhumane and they are deployed to deal with threats that risk the death of thousands or even hundreds of thousands.

One of these virtues code named T██████ was decommissioned after they had failed to execute an anomaly, which required the deployment of 2 other Virtues [[Hope]] and [[Fortitude]] to execute and the complete removal of the state ███ from written history and living memory. The virtue however left behind three “disciples” which would later find recognition among themselves as Virtues on a probationary term due to the suspicions concerning T██████’s influences. These virtues, now deployed after years of being on ice, are ready to help mentor the next generation of executioners.
