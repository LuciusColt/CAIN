Objectives:  
► Guide an ally to success.  
► **Pass the torch to someone new**.

Abilities:  
► **Over the Shoulder**: Allied executioners of your choice gain +1D to lead, intimidate, or give orders to anyone if you are standing adjacent to them.  
► **Experienced**: Once per mission if you tell someone how to do something and they succeed in doing it, they gain 1 XP.  
► **Measured Hand**: Once per rest you use Set-Up or Teamwork on an ally and they succeed, both of you relieve 1d3+1 stress.  
► **The Long Game**: Once per mission you may delay an ally’s hook from filling up. It becomes fully slashed after you both rest. If the Admin forgets to do this and you remind them, you and your ally gain 1 XP.  
► **Second Opinion**: Once per rest when an ally of choice fails a roll, they may reroll with +1D but still suffer previous consequences or risks.

>When you retire or die, without returning, executioners of your choice gain +1 XP.
