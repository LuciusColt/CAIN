Objectives:  
► Prosecute with extreme prejudice.  
► **Challenge your beliefs**.

Abilities:  
► **I, Distinguisher**: You always know when something directly in front of you is supernatural or mundane.  
► **Jury, Executioner**: If you reduce an execution tag to 1, you can take 2 stress to inflict +1 slash which cannot be reduced in any way.  
► **Exhibit A**: You gain +2D on rolls to convince another individual of something if you have some form of physical evidence pertaining to your argument.  
► **Broken Clocks Twice a Day**: After briefing at the start of deployment, make 1 specific claim or belief about the mission at hand. 1/scene gain +1D on actions to prove yourself right. If it is discovered you are correct, you lose this bonus but gain 1 burst.  
► **Weighted Judgment**: Once per rest when you publicly accuse or shame another individual of something serious, regain 1 burst.
