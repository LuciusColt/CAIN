## EXPANDED ANOMALIES

**Anomalies** are the stains of humanity to which executioners have a duty to wipe off the face of the earth. Over the years they have been successful in this role, but the recent steady and inevitable rise of anomaly manifestation rates has given birth to **new threats**.

Two new manifestation types have arisen in the past years, their true identity lost to misclassification and the hasty assumption that only the 6 primary anomalies were possible manifestations in anomaly hosts. The **Spider** is an anomaly born from powerlessness and the desire to take control over everything around them. The **Butcher** is an anomaly born from agony, whether physical or mental, and desires to inflict nothing but pain on everything around them.

>**TRIGGER WARNING**  
>The Butcher Anomaly contains narrative descriptions of gore and intense body horror.

Handlers are advised to exercise extreme caution when sending executioner squads to execute these threats due to their unprecedented potential and new challenges they pose to executioners beyond traditional hunts. Catalogue the data that your team finds in the field to report back to **TEMERITY**. Each day a new enemy arises. Do what your kind does best. No matter how many times they show up, kill them again, and again, and **again**.
