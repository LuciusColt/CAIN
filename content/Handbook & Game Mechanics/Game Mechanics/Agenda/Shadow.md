---
title: "[ SHADOW ]"
---
Objective:  
► Outshine your rival  
► **Let your rival outshine you**  

Abilities:  
► **Catch Up**: If your rival has less damage than you when pressure increases, erase 1 damage. If they have more bursts, regain 1 burst.  
► **Synchronize**: When you set up your rival, you take 1 nonlethal damage and gain +1D on the action to set up.  
► **You Can’t Die Now**: If your rival would take an injury or suffer instant death, you can take 2d3 damage to allow them to ignore it (if at max damage, they go to 1 damage under maximum) Then, lose the use of this ability until you rest.  
► **Shadow Seed**: Choose a defiance power your rival has. You can use that power this hunt, but only at max CAT 0.  
► **Pincer Technique**: When you participate in teamwork with your rival, you take 1 nonlethal stress, but can also re-roll one of the dice once, taking the second result as final.  

> When you pick this agenda, pick another character to be your rival (the feeling doesn’t have to be mutual) at the start of a mission. As long as you have rival abilities from this agenda, you must choose who your rival is.
