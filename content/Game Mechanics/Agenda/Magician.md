Objective:  
► Demonstrate flashy displays of power  
► **Invite catastrophe**  

Abilities:  
► **Perfect Technique**: Pick a defiance. Once a hunt, you can increase the CAT of that defiance by +1 temporarily for an entire scene, or for two rounds if it’s a conflict scene.  
► **Cantrip**: Pick a defiance ability. You can use it three times a hunt without spending a burst, but all parameters are at a max of CAT 0 if used this way.  
► **Finishing Move**: Pick a defiance power. You can increase the dice and CAT of that power by +1 the next time you use it, but you then cannot use any defiance again and all actions are hard. Both these effects end when you rest.  
► **Mimic technique**: Pick a power from any defiance, without having taken that defiance. It doesn’t change your xp or strain overflow caps.  
► **Weave**: When you use a defiance, you may increase the CAT of the next defiance used by an ally by +1, and grant it +1D on any rolls. Then lose the use of this ability until you rest.
