## **SECTION XV — TRAINING & SIMULATION FACILITY (“THE GLASS THEATERS”)**

**Document Reference:** W12–L3–SIM–056
**Location:** Level 3, Sub-Dome Sector
**Operational Status:** Active; Continuous Schedule

---

### **I. OVERVIEW**

The **Glass Theaters** comprise the main combat and scenario training division of Watchsite-12.
Here, Executioner candidates undergo both virtual and live-environment simulations designed to replicate field encounters with hostile anomalies.
These sessions serve dual purposes: **tactical conditioning** and **resonance monitoring.**

Though labeled as *training*, all exercises are treated as live data collection events.
Candidates who fail to maintain cognitive integrity are immediately reassigned to the Psychological Division—or removed from duty entirely.

> **Director Arctus:** “The field won’t forgive hesitation. Better they break here, where we can clean up after.”

---

### **II. FACILITY STRUCTURE**

**Layout:**

* **Primary Arena (T-1):** 50m x 50m open space with modular terrain tiles, holographic projection emitters, and kinetic resistance flooring.
* **Secondary Arenas (T-2 through T-5):** Variable geometry rooms for small-unit coordination or environmental hazard training (urban, forest, arctic, subterranean).
* **Observation Gallery:** Elevated control room encased in reinforced glass. Staff, analysts, and instructors observe from behind tinted panes.

The lighting system is capable of complete blackout or simulated weather.
Sound design includes environmental ambience and psychological triggers (e.g., crying, static, or heartbeat loops).

**Temperature Control:** Adjustable from −30 °C to +50 °C.

---

### **III. SIMULATION TYPES**

1. **Holo-Sim (HS):**

   * Non-lethal projection environments using neural-feedback visors.
   * Measures reaction time, fear responses, and command obedience.

2. **Hybrid Engagement (HE):**

   * Holographic environment with live physical threats (animatronic or captured lesser anomalies).
   * Monitors coordination, damage control, and moral reaction.

3. **Resonance Triggers (RT):**

   * Designed specifically for Executioners and Hosts to test control of their Grace.
   * May include emotionally charged reconstructions of trauma events.
   * Requires Dr. Hart or equivalent psychological officer present by protocol—but exemptions are frequently granted by Director Arctus.

4. **Finalization Trial (“The Glass Test”):**

   * Unscheduled. Candidates receive no briefing.
   * Survival and extraction determine advancement.
   * Mortality rate: 18%.

---

### **IV. STAFF & SUPERVISION**

**Lead Instructor:** *Captain Rho Varn* — Former field Executioner.
Hardline disciplinarian; noted for viewing trainees as “necessary assets, not people.”
Known to refer to the facility as *“a forge, not a nursery.”*

**Assistant Trainers:** Three rotating field veterans; none hold psychological credentials.
Dr. Hart’s petitions for increased mental health oversight remain “under review.”

Observers include Behavioral Analysts, Resonance Technicians, and occasionally outside auditors from CAIN Command.

---

### **V. TRAINEE EXPERIENCE**

Each day begins with a call over the intercom:

> “Candidate, report to Theater Three. Prepare for engagement.”

Candidates suit up in padded uniforms, sensors embedded along the spine and wrists. The floor vibrates with each power cycle, the hum of machines beneath like a distant heartbeat.

Once inside, they face illusions so real the line between training and mission dissolves:
rooms full of whispering shadows, faces in smoke, bleeding light.
Pain feedback is limited, but the fear is not.

Lucian’s logs show stable performance in Holo-Sims but marked distress during Hybrid Engagements.
During one RT session replicating the “Campus Incident,” he froze for nine seconds — an eternity in combat terms — then collapsed from neural feedback surge.

Captain Varn’s remark:

> “Better to burn weakness out now. The world outside eats slower.”

---

### **VI. CASUALTY RECORDS**

Watchsite-12 maintains a **casualty acceptance policy**:
*If a candidate dies in training, the data must justify the loss.*

Records show an average of two fatalities per quarter cycle, usually cardiac arrest, resonance overload, or environmental miscalibration.
Bodies are handled quietly through Level-8 transport tunnels. There is no ceremony—only a reassigned bunk and an updated roster.

Residents whisper that the Mirror Garden trees grow greener after someone dies below, though this remains unconfirmed.

---

### **VII. PSYCHOLOGICAL EFFECTS**

Reactions vary:

* **Compliant candidates** emerge disciplined but hollow, speaking in clipped, procedural language.
* **Empathic candidates** show higher breakdown rates but stronger field retention—Dr. Hart’s data point.
* **Lucian (X072):** Displays emotional detachment post-session, coupled with sketching activity. Possibly sublimation.

Hart’s note:

> “He fights like he’s apologizing. That’s rare. Maybe useful.”

---

### **VIII. ATMOSPHERE & DETAIL**

The halls around the Theaters smell faintly of ozone and sterilized sweat.
A continuous hum fills the air — machinery, ventilation, electricity.
Walls are painted neutral white-gray, but streaks of shoe rubber mark the corners.

The Observation Gallery windows never reflect the trainees clearly, only the faint blue glow of projection light.
Those who survive often describe feeling watched not just by staff, but by *something* in the mirrored glass itself.

Between sessions, trainees wait in silence on polymer benches, palms flat on their knees, eyes vacant.
The sound of the next door opening is met with relief or dread, depending on who you ask.

---

### **IX. CULTURAL AND INFORMAL PRACTICES**

* Trainees refer to survival of The Glass Test as “*earning your shadow*.”
* Memorial markings for lost candidates appear on locker undersides, carved initials hidden from inspection.
* Captain Varn forbids open mourning: *“We bury data, not bodies.”*
* Dr. Hart counteracts with quiet acts of defiance — a folded crane left in an empty locker, a report filed as “incomplete” to keep a name in circulation.

---
