### **DORMITORIES & PERSONAL QUARTERS (“THE ROW”)**
---
###### ***🜃 “As Above, So Below.”***
---
**Document Reference**: W12-L2-HAB-031
**Location**: Level 2 – Residential Wing East
**Access**: Restricted to resident-class personnel, monitored under Protocol 12-Sleep

##### **I. OVERVIEW**
The Residential Wing, informally called “The Row,” houses all Executioner candidates and recovered hosts assigned to Watchsite-12.
Each occupant is provided a private room engineered to balance comfort with containment.
Every door bears a soft-lit panel displaying the resident’s XID and biometric status (green = stable, yellow = anomalous, red = lockdown).
Corridors are narrow and warmly lit; the flooring muffles footsteps. The steady hiss of air circulation replaces silence.

##### **II. ROOM SPECIFICATIONS**
**Dimensions**: 4 m × 6 m average
**Construction**: Reinforced polymer composite with thermal insulation; walls colored neutral cream.
**Temperature**: Constant 22 °C; adjustable within ±2 °C.
**Standard Furnishings**:
* Single bed with memory-foam mattress and restraint-capable frame (unused in standard conditions).
* Writing desk and stool anchored to floor.
* Bookshelf (two shelves, transparent polymer).
* One storage locker integrated into wall.
* Personal sanitation module (sink, shower, mirror-screen).
* Observation viewport measuring 1.5 m × 1 m — triple-pane transparent alloy. View shows sub-ice horizon or the faint blue of the Garden lights depending on location.
**Lighting**: Indirect, dimmable. Lights fade automatically at curfew (22:00).
**Surveillance**: Ceiling-mounted sensor cluster monitors vital signs and auditory anomalies.
Camera feed is filtered for privacy; video accessible only under investigative review.

##### **III. PERSONALIZATION POLICY**
Residents are permitted limited customization:
* Two personal objects (books, photos, drawings).
* Non-metallic decorative materials (paper, fabric, polymer).
* One plant cutting from the Mirror Garden, issued on request and logged.
Unauthorized items are confiscated during weekly inspection.
Despite restrictions, rooms subtly reflect each occupant’s personality—folded paper animals, pinned sketches, handwritten quotes on issued stationery.

##### **IV. LUCIAN (X072) — QUARTER SUMMARY**
Lucian’s room is positioned on the outer ring, facing the glacier wall.
At night, reflected aurora light drifts through the viewport, painting the interior pale blue.
**Contents (authorized):**

* Copy of *The Stranger* (dog-eared).
* Pressed leaf from Mirror Garden maple (in sealed pouch).
**Behavioral Notes:**

##### **V. SOCIAL AND ROUTINE ELEMENTS**
The Row is quiet; most communication occurs through knocks or short paper notes slipped under doors.
Residents rarely visit one another’s quarters, but they share an unspoken ritual: placing a small object outside their door on nights before simulation tests—tokens of luck or apology.
Weekly, during cleaning rotation, doors open simultaneously for twenty minutes.
During that time, faint music from multiple rooms merges into a collective, echoing hum.

##### **VI. SANITATION & MAINTENANCE**
Laundry and linens are exchanged every seven days via automated chute.
Showers activate by biometric scan; water temperature fixed at 38 °C.
Mirror-screens auto-dim after 90 seconds of continuous gaze to prevent reflective fixation incidents.
All maintenance occurs while residents are at training or therapy; returned rooms smell faintly of citrus sterilizer and ozone.
