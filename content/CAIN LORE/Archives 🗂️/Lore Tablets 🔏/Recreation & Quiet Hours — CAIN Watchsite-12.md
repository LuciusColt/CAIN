### **I. OVERVIEW**

The **Mirror Garden** is Watchsite-12’s largest open space and its primary morale-sustainment environment.

Humidity hovers at 42%, temperature at 21 °C, and faint ionization in the air produces the scent of rain even when none falls.

Residents refer to it simply as **“the Garden.”**

### **II. LAYOUT & FEATURES**

The dome roof filters the outside light; during daylight it glows in muted blues, at night it opens to reveal the aurora. When storms strike, snow whirls across the glass in white ribbons, making the space feel like the inside of a heartbeat.
