##### **I. STANDARD ISSUE UNIFORM**
All held individuals at Watchsite-12 are provided standardized clothing designed for psychological neutrality, warmth retention, and environmental adaptability.
**Uniform Components**:
* **Footwear**:
  * “One-size” adaptive polymer shoes, matte black with soft soles.
  * Automatically conform to wearer’s shape after 60 seconds of wear.
  * Purpose: minimize noise and prevent slip hazards on reinforced flooring.
* **Pants**:
  * Color: Ash grey.
  * Material: Insulated cotton-blend fleece, resistant to static charge.
  * Cut: Relaxed fit with elastic waistband and drawcord.
  * Pockets: Single rear pocket (right side only).
  * Identifier: Serial code (XID) printed vertically along outer seam in faint silver thread.
  * *Lucian’s designated XID: X072.*
* **Shirt / Top**:
  * Material: Dual-layer quilted fabric — breathable and body heat reflective.
  * Design: Long-sleeved, slightly oversized to reduce visual aggression profile.
  * Pockets: Two front hand-level pockets placed ¾ down the torso — one left, one right. Each can hold easily pocket-sized objects (e.g., notebooks, gloves, cards, small books).
  * Identification: XID printed on left breast above pocket.
  * Color: Light slate grey with thin white piping along seams.
The ensemble is intentionally *non-uniform in texture but uniform in appearance*—producing a communal sense of belonging while limiting social hierarchy among residents.

##### **II. PSYCHOLOGICAL RATIONALE**
According to the Cognitive Division, the Watchsite-12 resident uniform serves as a “comfort aesthetic: a deliberate visual deterrent to fear and authority.”
The aim is to prevent escalation, encourage compliance, and cultivate calm introspection.
* The color grey was selected after a six-month behavioral study showed it reduced “reactionary episodes” by 18%.
* The puffed fabric mimics the tactile safety of domestic winter wear, triggering subconscious associations with warmth and familiarity.
* The limited pocket count minimizes concealment opportunities while maintaining dignity and a sense of autonomy.
* The XID placement allows for easy identification without overtly resembling a prisoner number — reinforcing belonging without humiliation.

##### **III. PERSONAL GROOMING PROTOCOLS**
All residents must maintain a clean and orderly appearance.
Facilities provide scheduled access to sanitation and grooming under observation by attendant staff.
**Schedule**:
* **Showers**: Twice daily (morning and night), five-minute duration.
* **Laundry**: Weekly cycle; residents exchange clothing for freshly sanitized issue.
* **Haircuts**: Every two weeks, or upon request within regulation limits.
* **Shaving / Hygiene**: Razors are single-use, issued during supervised intervals, and immediately collected.
Hair may be styled or grown within reasonable limits, provided it does not obstruct facial identification scans or interfere with simulation training gear.
>[[Lucian]], for example, maintains medium-length hair swept aside, as recorded in profile [[public/Archives-🗂️/Miscellaneous-Info-🎲/Photos-🎥/X072–VIS–PHOT–04.png]]. Despite this, post-training reports note temporary hair elongation during resonance events — tolerated under exemption clause 12-C.

##### **IV. REPLACEMENT & MAINTENANCE**
Uniforms are recycled and refitted in the L-2 Fabrication Ward, where low-risk residents assist in repair and cleaning under supervision.
Garments are heat-treated to sterilize biological contaminants, including trace resonance particulate.
Damage to issued attire (tears, scorch marks, or contamination) must be reported immediately. Replacement is automated via dispensary system.
Failure to return used garments results in disciplinary review under Protocol W12–CONDUCT–14.

##### **V. ADDITIONAL NOTES**
* Outerwear (coats, gloves, or scarves) is permitted only during excursions into the upper biosphere or transit tunnels.
* Colors other than grey require Level-3 authorization (typically white for medical personnel, blue for instructors).
* Uniforms are prohibited from personalization. Attempts to embroider, alter fit, or modify texture trigger confiscation and reissuance.
