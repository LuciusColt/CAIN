## PERFECT OGRE

*You see it too? For me, it’s always like this...*

---

**Suffocate**: Executioners cannot rest inside the miasma for any reason, nor can they use any kit with the consumable note inside. 

**Perfect Decay**: Any stress taken from the ogre or its traces on a 1-3 reaction grants the rotting affliction (see afflictions in [[12.1. OGRE]]). 

**Sunkiller**: The Perfect Ogre deals +1 stress with reactions to executioners with an injury. 

**Field of the Limbless**: Pulverize rips off a limb for EACH 1 rolled, instead of just a single limb if two or more ones are rolled. If an executioner has 3 or more limbs ripped off, they also suffer instant death.
