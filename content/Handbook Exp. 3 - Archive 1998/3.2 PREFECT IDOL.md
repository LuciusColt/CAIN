## PERFECT IDOL

>Poor things. Needless suffering. Too much **thinking**...

---

**Mock**: The Idol’s base execution tag is 6 instead of 10. However, at the start of the hunt, the Idol may ask the following trauma questions to any executioner. During a conflict scene, the Idol may use the answers to counter an executioner’s action, reducing slashes taken by 1d3 and inflicting the same amount of stress on the triggering executioner. Each answer can only be used once.

1. Are you human?
2. When did you realize you were a tool?
3. What precious thing have you lost forever?
   
**Perfect Dream**: When the executioners rest, two executioners of the Idol’s choice gain an idol affliction. 

**Perfect Divinity**: Extreme Agony cannot be invoked against the Idol.

**Ritual of Flesh**: Marriage can target and fuse 3 executioners, and inflicts 1d3 strain on each.
