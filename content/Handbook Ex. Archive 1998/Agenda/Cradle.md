Objective:  
► Protect your ward from the consequences of their actions.  
► **Let your ward suffer.**  

>When you choose this agenda, have one of its powers, or have its bolded item, choose another character as your ward. You can switch your ward around between hunts.

Abilities:  
► **Matriphagy**: Your ward can deal 3 nonlethal but irreducible stress to you in place of a mental burst as long as they are in short range of you.  
► **Coddle**: Once a conflict scene, you may take the defend or aid action for free, targeting only your ward. This doesn’t take your action for the round.  
► **Fragile**: You gain +2 max stress, but can no longer clear stress by any means other than taking an injury.  
► **Pain Eater**: Once a hunt, when you give your ward a warm gesture (a smile, a huge, a pat on the head), you can transfer any amount of stress and up to one affliction or hook from them to you. This could cause an injury or kill you.  
► **Offering**: As long as you are in short range from your ward, you can gain 1d3+1 strain to prevent any injury to your ward, or 1d6+2 to prevent instant death. Each can trigger separately only once a hunt. Your ward instead remains at 1 stress below max. Describe how you avert disaster.
