## MOTHER'S TREASURE

Use these foes if you want to fight Mother. Mother is a reoccurring problem in current and abandoned Temerity facilities.

Several reactions from fighting Mother can infect executioners with her. Gaining her this way doesn’t lower strain cap, doesn’t immediately grant a power, and doesn’t raise XP cap like gaining a new defiance normally would. Otherwise, treat her like a normal defiance.

- **MOTHER'S SWEET**
	
	**Anomaly**. Execution tag 2 (solo), 6 (swarm)  
	CAT 0-4 
	
	Relatively weak, result of colonization of the graceless. Act in perfect synchronicity around other parts of Mother. 
	
	**Reactions**:  
	
	**Inflict harm**. Attacks with: Spiny extrusions, venomous bite (1): 3 stress and 1 sin. (2/3): 2 stress (4+): 2 stress. 
	
	**Or**: Threat (1-2: Join - Swarm Only): Erase up to 4 slashes on execution tag if successful, then become a Mother’s Dear, replacing all reactions. 
	
	**Or**: Threat (1-2: Colonize): Jab with sharp spine. If successful, kills self, but then deals 5 stress to its target and infects that executioner with Mother.

- **MOTHER'S DEAR**
	
	**Anomaly**. Execution tag 4 (solo), 8 (duo)  
	CAT 1-4 
	
	High priority target for execution. Result of multiple graceless subjects fusing. Very slow, but methodical. Barely flinches when struck. Has no individual sense of preservation.
	
	**Armored**: takes -1 slash from direct attacks of equal or lower category unless armor is dealt with through setup or circumstance (finding weak points, cracking with overwhelming force, etc).
	
	**Reactions**:
	
	**Inflict harm**. Attacks with: Carapace, horned headbutt, retractable spines (1): 4 stress and smashed rib affliction (physical exertion of any kind is hard). (2/3): 3 stress (4+): 2 stress.
	
	**Or**: Threat (1-2: Colonize): Jab with sharp spine. If successful, kills self, but then deals 5 stress to its target and infects that executioner with Mother.
