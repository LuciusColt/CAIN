## PERFECT LORD

>Open the Gates, and I will reveal to you the Kingdom...

---

**Perfect Kingdom**: Resting anywhere causes you to enter or exit the Lord’s Kingdom (it’s choice). 

**Immutable Control**: When the lord creates a complication, it takes +2 more slashes to deal with (this includes the free complications from being a Perfect Anomaly). 

**Perfect Purity**: Executioners gain +1 strain when gaining strain inside the kingdom. 

**Immaculate Prosecution**: Judgement allows no other executioners to aid.
