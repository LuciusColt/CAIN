## PERFECT TOAD

>Nice toys. I think I’ll take them...

---

**Perfect Larceny**: The Toad steals 1d3 resting dice from the executioners when they rest and may roll and spend them to heal. 

**Get a New Trick**: Once a round, as a 1-3 reaction in a conflict scene, the Toad may seal an executioner’s defiance power. That power may not be used this scene again without first taking 1d3+1 strain each time. 

**Perfect Spawn**: Toadspawn are Anomalies and deal +1 stress on all reactions. 

**Shadow of the True Self**: Grand Finesse's shadow execution tag gains '+ CAT' and '+ PRESSURE' length and it now becomes a combatant (stat as a binder, see [[14. BINDERS]]). It can react alongside the anomaly once a round for free, like a Trace.
