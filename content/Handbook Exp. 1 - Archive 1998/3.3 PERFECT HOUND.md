## PERFECT HOUND

>I alone will stand...

---

**Perfect Reflex**: The hound takes two free reactions at the start of the first round of any conflict scene (still roll 1d6 for each). 

**Berserk**: At 50% tag or less, deals +1 stress with all reactions, and may use a threat to inflict instant death with a 1-3 reaction. 

**Perfect Body**: When executioners rest, the hound heals to full on its execution tag. 

**Blood Sated Sword Soul**: Every executioner flying to aid another executioner targeted by Apoplexy takes +1 stress and a flay scar. All humans in a CAT sized area centered on the hound are instantly slaughtered.
