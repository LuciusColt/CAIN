## WEAPONS

An Arsenal of weaponry is machined and maintained by CASTLE armory services. Some improvised in the field. You may choose the aesthetic of your service weapon. They start at CAT 0. You may upgrade both of them by +1 CAT by spending 3 scrip, to a max of CAT 3. All these weapons have been lovingly used to kill anomalies.
