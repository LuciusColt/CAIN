Objective:  
► Follow Orders  
► **Disobey Orders**  

Abilities:  
► **Black Suit**: Gain +1D on actions to lead, intimidate, or give orders to mundane humans.  
► **Mea Culpa**: The first time in a hunt you take an injury or an affliction, relieve 1d3+1 strain.  
► **Eliminate the Stain**: Gain +1D and inflict +1 slash on tags the first time you roll against anomalies that have half or less of their execution tag remaining.  
► **Single Minded**: You can only be affected by one affliction maximum (you can choose which to discard and keep).  
► **By the Book**: Once a hunt, when you witness or find evidence of an anomalies’ Domain, you can force the Admin to show you the exact rules text of that Domain.
