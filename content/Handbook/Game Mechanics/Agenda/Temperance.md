Objective:  
► Put people before the mission  
► **Harm someone intentionally**  

Abilities:  
► **Healer**: The first time you rest in a hunt, gain an extra resting die.  
► **Focused**: Once a hunt, you may use a defiance that affects only you or your allies without spending a burst, and it gains +1 CAT.  
► **Savior Complex**: Gain +1D on actions to directly prevent or avoid harm to humans.  
► **Care**: Once a hunt, outside of a conflict scene, you can roll and apply a resting die to yourself or another nearby ally without resting.  
► **Gentleness**: For one scene, you may take 1 less stress when you take stress. This ability breaks if you take any forceful or harmful action. Then lose the use of this ability until you rest.
