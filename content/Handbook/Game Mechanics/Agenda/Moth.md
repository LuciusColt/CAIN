Objective:  
► Uncover hidden or uncomfortable truths about the world.  
► **Uncover hidden or uncomfortable truths about yourself.**  

Abilities:  
► **Psyche Jewel**: When you or an ally answers a anomaly's trauma question, regain 1 burst, recover 1 health, and erase 1 strain.  
► **Alienation**: Your strain overflow cap increases by +3.  
► **Rapture**: You erase 1 damage when you gain an affliction, injury, or hook.  
► **Larval**: If you end a mission at 7 or higher strain, gain 1 xp. If you suffer strain overflow, gain 1 xp.  
► **Unveil**: You can ask the Admin about a character present ‘What is this person afraid of?’. Gain +1D when you act on the answers for the rest of the scene, then lose the use of this ability until you rest.
