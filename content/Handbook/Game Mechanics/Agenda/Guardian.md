Objective:  
► Protect your people  
► **Leave nobody behind**  

Abilities:  
► **Iron Wall**: When you defend someone close to you in a conflict scene, you can choose to divert any consequences taken by your target on to you instead of its usual effects. The second time or more you use this in the same scene, you take 1d3 nonlethal damage first.  
► **The Excessive Agony of Moving Forward**: Erase 1 damage when the hour passes if you have more than 3.  
► **Castle**: When any ally takes an injury, you erase 1 damage and gain +1D on your next action. 
► **Centerweight**: When resting, allies that spend resting dice can set the results of their dice to yours instead.  
► **Painkiller**: Your maximum HEALTH is no longer reduced by taking injuries.  

> Note: You don't get EXP or SCRIP for the dead.
