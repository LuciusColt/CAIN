Objective:  
► Enrich yourself  
► **Give something valuable away**

Abilities:  
► **Black Market Stims**: You can spend up to 3 scrip a mission as a burst.  
► **Supplier Connection**: You gain a 1 scrip discount on all items, to a minimum of 1. This discount increases to 2 if you have survived 5 or more missions.  
► **Shady Lender**: Between missions, you can choose to gain 1d3 scrip but start the next mission at 1 injury.  
► **Scrounger**: You gain +1 scrip for completing missions, no matter what. You never get docked scrip for failing missions.  
► **Spotless**: Once a mission, you can destroy an aesthetic kit expansion you own worth 3 or more scrip instead of suffering an injury.  

>You can spend scrip as kit points
