CASE NAME: *FETID NECROPOLIS*  
CODE: #2120  

ANOMALY CODENAME: *THE TOIL OF UNFINISHED WORKS*
FORM: TYPE 1 / SEVERED  

TRIGGER WARNINGS: **Burnout, Overworking, Workplace Harassment, Body Horror**  
COMPLEXITY: 3  

IDENTIFIED ANOMALY TYPE: **CENTIPEDE**  
IDENTIFIED ANOMALY CATEGORY: **4**  

TRAUMA QUESTIONS:  
**Q1**. What are you trying to escape?  
 **A1**. The hopeless realization that I will _never_ make my dream game.
   
  **DOMAIN 1**: 
  - GENTLY ROLLING DOWN THE SLOPES OF THE ABYSS
  -> The Centipede’s venom is so potent and fast spreading that it begins to cause catastrophic spread.
  
  • Double the casualties per pressure (to 20%). At full segments (6), the spread begins to go outside of the quarantine zone, requiring cleanup from CAIN and docking the executioners 2 scrip.
  
  • When the players meet an NPC, the Admin makes a 1d6 fortune roll. On a ‘1-3’, they have already been bitten but are trying to hide it.
  
  • As a **tension move**, the Admin can choose an uninfected executioner or NPC who has fought the infested at least once this mission. The Admin reveals they have been bitten, giving them a Centipede Bite.
  
**Q2**. What do you hate the most about humanity?  
 **A2**. Bullies, people who think you're *inferior* to them. 
  
  **DOMAIN 2**: 
  - I HIDE MY KNIFE IN THE SOFT SUPPLE WALLS
  ->The Centipede is able to set festering traps for the executioners, extruding parts of its body or using the transformed flesh of its infested horde.
  
  As a **tension move**, the Admin can declare that for the next scene, the executioners are about to enter a trapped area (even if they have already explored it). Moving through the area becomes risky by default. Traps can: inflict 1 stress to one or two executioners, make an area dangerous or hard to move through, afflict an executioner, inflict a hook. In addition, in any conflict scene with the Centipede, on a ‘1’ risk result a trap also goes off, with the above effects, in addition to any other reactions.

**Q3**. What do you regret the most?  
 **A3**. Prioritizing the *game* over the person I love.  
 
  **DOMAIN 3**: 
  - AT THE CORE, MY ROT UNFURLS
  -> The Centipede is so fueled by spite it refuses to die and instead takes a momentarily more powerful form.
  
  When the centipede’s execution clock is filled up, it instead reduces by 4. The centipede then takes on a terrifying final form.
  
  • End the current round and start a new round. 
  
  • In this form, roll the risk die twice and choose the lower result, the centipede takes -1 slash on its tags and deals +1 stress with all its actions. 
  
  • However, this form is extremely unstable. At the end of the round, after all executioners have acted, no matter what, the centipede’s form destabilizes, instantly defeating it and allowing the executioners the opportunity to finish it off.

**OTHER INFORMATION**  
SEVERE ATTACK - **VITRIOL**: The Anomaly can use this ability on a ‘1’ on the risk roll. They can only use it once a mission.

**Target an Executioner**. Any other executioners nearby must decide to fly to their aid. Any that don’t offer aid cannot participate. Start with a pool of 6d6. Then remove one dice for each of the following. If an answer is ‘no’, you or someone aiding you can immediately make a single action roll to attempt to rectify the answer, with only a few moments to act, suffering consequences as normal if they fail.

• Can you move quickly and unencumbered?  
• Is someone aiding you able to push or grab you?  
• Can you forgive the centipede’s host?  
• Is the centipede hindered, distracted, or under duress in some way?  

Then roll the dice.

The centipede shoots a pressurized stream of mutagenic venom at the targeted executioner, dissolving all obstacles and flesh in its way with incredible force. The executioner and any aiding them take 1 stress for every die rolled, no matter the result.

If at least one ‘1’ comes up, the targeted executioner suffers an injury and rolls on the table for a permanent scar. If two or more ‘1’s come up, the executioner targeted can either:

• Suffer instant death.  
• Suffer strain overflow to avoid instant death.  
• Suffer an injury and roll three times on the table instead for permanent scars.

*Vitriol Scars* 
1. Missing eye, fused shut. 
2. Large bleached patch of skin. 
3. Fingers on one hand melted together. 
4. Missing hair, burn scar on scalp. 
5. Massive burn scar over both arms.
6. Rippling acid burn from neck to groin.

**ATTACKS**:
- (1) **VITRIOL** (only once) or Bite 2 with Fangs. 5 Damage.
- (2/3): Slash, or Cut with Legs. 3 Damage.
- (4+): Wrap up an Executioner. 2 Damage.

**COMPLICATION**:
- Hides behind various large tombstones and monoliths, making hitting the anomaly hard. 
-  Summons lesser minions of the horde and feasts on their flesh to regain strength.
-  The area is covered in graves, tombstones, etc., making movement through the terrain difficult.

**THREATS**:
- Summon the Horde, Summon 1d6+1 Traces.
- Spew Venom at 1d3 Executioners.
- Wrap up an executioner and try to bite off one of their limbs or swallow them whole.

| Limbs     | 1D6 |
| --------- | --- |
| Head      | 1   |
| Swallowed | 2   |
| Leg       | 3-4 |
| Arm       | 5-6 |

**AFFLICTIONS**:
1. Target an Executioner. For every '6' that Executioner rolls they take 1 Nonlethal Damage.
2. Permanently add to your Agenda '**Kill Needlessly**'. 
3. CENTIPEDE BITE:
   - This hook counts as an affliction that you can *never remove*. It ticks up and down all the normal ways that hooks tick.
   - If the hook fills up to full, take 1d3+1 damage and strain immediately, taking damage again whenever pressure increases while the hook is full.
   - The hook can be ticked down with centipede anti-venom.
   >Note: Every executioner comes equipped with 1 vial of anti-venom. 1 Charge, 1 KP, Consumable.
