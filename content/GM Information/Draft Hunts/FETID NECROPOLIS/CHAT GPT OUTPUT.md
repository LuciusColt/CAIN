**ANOMALY TYPE:** Centipede  
**HOST:** Peter Gravewind _(placeholder last name; gothic & thematic—feel free to adjust)_  
**THREAT:** Containment-critical. Corruption only inside office interior _for now_, but showing early signs of spatial bleed.

---

# **I. MISSION BRIEF (WADE → Players)**

WADE gives them **three initial leads**, strongly recommending they investigate at least 2 before setting foot in the corrupted office:

### **Initial Locations (the 3 the players know about):**

1. **Cryptworks Studios HQ** _(the office dungeon — available immediately but strongly discouraged)_
    
2. **Melissa’s Apartment Block** _(Peter’s ex-girlfriend)_
    
3. **Starlit Cup Café** _(Peter’s post-work refuge; location of a key secondary NPC)_
    

These three create the “starting triangle.”

Each location opens 1–2 additional “linked nodes,” forming a simple branching path without overwhelming the players.

WADE also gives them the **three trauma questions** but redacted, saying:

> “Peter Gravewind. Centipede candidate. Cracked under pressure.  
> To kill this thing, you have to understand him. The anomaly came from three wounds — figure those wounds out.”

# **II. TRAUMA QUESTIONS + WHERE ANSWERS ARE FOUND**

Your players uncover each answer by exploring **one primary and one secondary location**.

### **QUESTION 1 — “What are you trying to escape?”**

**Answer:** Corporate exploitation, creative suffocation, and the hopeless realization he will _never_ make his dream game.

**Found through:**

- **Starlit Cup Café** (Main)
    
- **Cryptworks Storage Archive** (Linked from Café)
    

---

**Answer:** That people trivialize dreams. That everyone seems satisfied with mediocrity and mock earnest creative passion.

**Found through:**

- **Cryptworks Studios (Office Floor 1)** (Main)
    
- **HR Director Callahan’s Deleted Inbox / VoiceLogs** (Linked from Office Floor 1)
    

---

### **QUESTION 3 — “What do you regret the most?”**

**Answer:** Working himself to the bone and driving away Melissa — prioritizing the game over the person he loved.

**Found through:**

- **Meeting Melissa** (Main)
    
- **Peter’s Apartment** (Linked from Melissa)

# **III. LOCATION WEB (Simple Branching)**

## **1. Cryptworks Studios (Main Dungeon Node)**

_Downtown Manhattan. Exterior: normal. Interior: full JRPG necropolis corruption._

When entering, players find:

- **Peter’s desk** (charred, reshaped as a JRPG “save point shrine”)
    
- A **bloodied elevator** with corrupted signage

**Clue Provided Here (unlocking Linked Node):**

- Office gossip logs showing Peter being mocked in group chats
    
- Email chains ridiculing _Fetid Necropolis_
    
- Corporation announcing the cancellation of his project

# **IV. OFFICE DUNGEON STRUCTURE (JRPG-Turned-Horror)**

### **Floor 1: Lobby → Graveyard Hub Zone**

- Corrupted cubicles = mausoleums
    
- Traces: “Forgotten Warriors”—zombie interns, skeletons with office wear
    
- Puzzle: activate three “lantern obelisks” to unlock elevator
    

---

### **Floor 2: Mini-Boss — _THE ROOK LICH_**

A classic JRPG undead knight archetype, twisted and disgustingly corporate:

**Appearance:**

- Giant suit of armor made of filing cabinets
    
- A skull made of staplers
    
- Rusty tower-shield with a quarterly-report graph carved into it
    
- Carries a bladed greatsword made of fused laptops
    

**Theme:**  
Represents **executive oversight** and the crushing managerial pressure on Peter.

**Arena:**  
The “Department of Valor & Compliance” — cubicles arranged like a JRPG battle map.

On defeat, the Rook Lich drops:

- **The Key of Overtime** (opens the boss floor)
    
- **A scrap of paper saying “I just wanted to make something that mattered.”**
    

---

### **Floor 3: Final Boss — The Centipede + Peter**

**The Centipede** is a colossal, rotting JRPG monster made of:

- Bones
    
- Armor
    
- Old prototypes
    
- Shattered office furniture
    
- Purple venom ooze dripping like corrupted UI elements
    

Peter sits inside its “skull-throne” section, unconscious, kept alive by the Centipede.

**Boss Mechanics:**  
The Centipede will scream the trauma answers during the fight _if players got them wrong_. If they got them **right**, the fight is significantly easier.

- WADE congratulates or reprimands them depending on their investigation path

# **I. PALACE MAP — FETID NECROPOLIS**

**Overall Structure:**  
A _vertical graveyard citadel_ collapsing into itself — like a city swallowed by a mausoleum.  
Each floor represents a stage in **Peter Delmont’s emotional decomposition**, turning his burnout, despair, and hatred into a rotting labyrinth.

Each floor name follows a pattern:  
**[Emotion / State] + [Corpse/Grave Imagery]**

---

## **FLOOR 1 — THE THRESHOLD OF QUIET MEAT**

_(Foyer / Entry Floor — still half “office,” half graveyard)_

- Flickering fluorescents dangle from exposed tendons.
    
- Printer trays spit out funeral pamphlets with unreadable names.
    
- Cubicles slump like **rotted coffins**.
    
- The walls pulse with slow centipede-like movement, as though the building is _breathing through dead lungs_.
    

**Enemies:**

- **Clerks of Stagnant Flesh** (office workers with jointed bone legs)
    
- **The Missed Deadline**, a floating mass of torn calendar pages and vertebrae
    

---

## **FLOOR 2 — THE SCRIPTORIUM OF ROTTEN INTENT**

_(Narrative design office turned ossuary chapel)_

- Storyboards on the walls depict **Peter’s abandoned game**, but characters appear replaced by skeletal larvae.
    
- Notes have been rewritten by the anomaly into hateful prayers:  
    _“May all things return to the soil.”_
    

**Enemies:**

- **Draft Wraiths** (unfinished narrative ideas hunting for an ending)
    
- **Concept Larvae** (tiny bone-centipedes with faces made from paper scraps)
    

---

## **FLOOR 3 — THE OSSUARY OF BROKEN EXPECTATION**

_(The management floor)_

- Conference tables are now surgical slabs.
    
- HR offices look like judge’s chambers built from ribcages.
    
- A looping, droning voice recites “productivity goals” like a funeral litany.
    

**Enemies:**

- **The Hollow Supervisor** (a skeleton wrapped in performance review sheets)
    
- **Corpse-Drones** (interns with centipede torsos and staplers embedded in their skulls)
    

---

## **FLOOR 4 — THE BURIAL ARCADE**

_(Game dev floor fully transformed into palace environment)_

- Rows of PCs fuse into tombstones.
    
- Screens flicker with purple ooze leaking from UI windows.
    
- A colossal server rack pulses like a **heart stitched from hard drives**.
    

**Enemies:**

- **The Scrapped Build** (a mass of corrupted code and bone fragments)
    
- **Lag Wraiths** (glitching corpses that blink unpredictably)
    

---

## **FLOOR 5 — SANCTUM OF THE FETID CENTIPEDE**

_(The Core / Throne Room)_

- A vast circular chamber made entirely of layered tombstones.
    
- A spiraling bone burrow bores down into a purple void.
    
- The air itself decays as you breathe it — leaving a film on your teeth.
    

**Boss:**

### **THE FETID CENTIPEDE — “Delmont’s Escape”**

A colossal, half-rotten bone centipede held together by sinew, purple bile, and pages of his script glued into its ribs.

# **III. BOSS OF THE PALACE — _THE FETID CENTIPEDE_**

### **Description**

A miles-long skeletal centipede coiled into the shape of a throne.  
Each segment is a coffin.  
Each limb is a sharpened femur.  
Its core glows with purple bile — a toxic sludge made from hatred, burnout, and the desire for everything to stop living.

It embodies:

- **Peter’s desire to escape through death**
    
- **His hatred for everyone who made him feel trapped**
    
- **His wish that the world would just rot away with him**
    

---

## **SPECIAL ATTACKS (3)**

### **1. → “Hatred That Crawls”**

The centipede sheds dozens of bone-segments that become independent crawling enemies.  
Each one whispers something Peter wishes he’d said to people he hated.  
Damage scales the more trauma questions remain unanswered.

### **2. → “Rotting Dream Exhale”**

Breathes a cloud of purple vapor made from the dissolved remains of his game project.  
Anyone inhaling it experiences **visions of being buried alive inside someone else’s expectations**.

Applies Disorientation + Psychic Bleed.

### **3. → “Escape Through Death”**

The centipede attempts to burrow _through reality itself_, creating a rupture.  
If successful, the palace begins expanding outside the building.  
This is effectively a **mission fail-state timer**.

Players must stop the attack by exploiting answers to trauma questions.

# **V. THEMING DOCUMENT — FETID NECROPOLIS**

Below is a **concise reference sheet** to keep everything unified.

---

## **THE THEMATIC TRIAD**

The entire mission is built on three themes:

### **1. Rot As Emotion**

Rot isn’t literal decay — it’s what happens to a person who can’t leave.

### **2. Death As Escape**

This isn’t a place obsessed with killing.  
It is a place where **death is the only route to freedom**.

### **3. Hatred As Fuel**

The anomaly’s core grows from **pure resentment** — not rage, but _resentment_ — the cold, wet hatred of wasted life.

---

## **PALETTE — VISUAL + TONE**

- **Colors:** Bone-white, bile-purple, bruise-black
    
- **Textures:** Wet stone, pulsing sinew, engraved bone, moldy paper
    
- **Vibe:** Dead museum + abandoned office + rotting MMO dungeon
    
- **Ambient Noise:**
    
    - Keyboard clicks that sound like mandibles
        
    - Footsteps like falling dirt
        
    - Distant centipede skittering
        
    - Whispered deadlines
        

---

## **ENEMY DESIGN PRINCIPLE**

> Every enemy is a _memory_, a _job responsibility_, or a _regret_ that refused to stay dead.

---

## **ENVIRONMENTAL LOGIC**

- Every floor used to be part of the office.
    
- Bones = architecture.
    
- Purple ooze = emotional residue.
    
- Sinew = the thing holding him together.
    
- Centipedes = movement without growth; life without life.
    

---

## **PETER DELMONT’S EMOTIONAL ARC**

### **Stage 1: Exhaustion**

He just wanted to rest.

### **Stage 2: Hatred**

He began to wish the world would stop so he could stop.

### **Stage 3: Collapse**

He put his hope in a game — but it, too, was taken.

### **Stage 4: Rot**

His inner world decomposed faster than his outer life.

### **Stage 5: Escape**

The anomaly is his attempt to crawl out of reality entirely.  
The Bone Centipede is the part of him that refuses to die quietly.

---

## **BOSS SYMBOLIC BREAKDOWN**

- **Centipede:** endless forward motion despite wanting to stop
    
- **Bone:** he feels hollow
    
- **Rot:** he feels used-up
    
- **Purple Bile:** emotional poison
    
- **Coffin Segments:** every part of him is already dead
    
- **Mandibles:** biting the world before it can hurt him again
