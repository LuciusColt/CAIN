Objectives:  
► Demonstrate why you’re the worst.  
► **Drag someone down to your level**.

Abilities:  
► **Schadenfreude**: 1/rest when you witness an ally’s affliction hook fill out or witness them experience intense suffering, relieve 1d3 stress. Relieve 2d3 instead if it was self inflicted.  
► **Heart of Copper**: 1/mission when you take needless, excessive, and cruel action against the innocent, relieve 1d3-1 strain (Min 1).  
► **Swindle**: You roll +1D on actions to deceive or manipulate others for your own personal gain.  
► **Leech**: Whenever an ally relieves strain or stress using a consumable you may reduce the amount relieved by 1 in order to relieve 1 stress and/or strain from yourself instead.  
► **Despised**: When fighting the anomaly and you roll a 1 on the risk die, it must target you with its severe attack if it hasn’t used it this mission.
