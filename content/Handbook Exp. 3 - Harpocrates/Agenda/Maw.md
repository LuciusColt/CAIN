Objectives:  
► Act upon your hunger.  
► **Turn down a chance to satisfy yourself.**

Abilities:  
► **Insatiable**: You may use two consumables at once as a part of the same action. Consuming them this way lowers the overall cost of KP by 1.  
► **Long Pig**: When you consume the flesh of a sentient being for the first time in a mission, regain 1 burst and heal 1d3 stress.  
► **Gullet**: You can eat much more than normal and eat most things without consequence. Poisons, objects, and inedible things do not harm you when eaten. Outright dangerous things such as explosives, hostile creatures, etc will still hurt you if eaten.  
► **The Late Worm**: Acting last in the round of any conflict scene lets you roll +1D to the first action you take during your turn.  
► **That Fleeting Taste**: Pick 1 vice such as sweets, cigarettes, etc. 1/rest when you consume your vice, relieve 1d3 stress and 1d3 strain. Every time you do the admin secretly rolls 2D and adds the results. On a 2-5, you no longer relieve stress or strain when you partake. On future rolls of 11 or 12, you regain the benefits of this effect. This repeats and the Admin only tells you if this ability works or not after each instance of partaking in your vice.
