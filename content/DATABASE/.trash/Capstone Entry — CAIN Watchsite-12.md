### **I. LOCATION & PURPOSE**

Watchsite-12 lies buried within the **Wohlthat Massif**, Antarctica, concealed beneath kilometers of glacial ice.
Its founding directive: *“To contain, study, and repurpose anomalous human manifestations toward the preservation of humanity.”*

It houses both **Executioner candidates** and **recovered hosts**, training them in controlled environments while maintaining ongoing research into anomalous resonance, artifact behavior, and cognitive resilience.
It also functions as long-term storage for objects too dangerous to destroy and too useful to lose.

### **V. OPERATIONAL PROFILE**

Watchsite-12 operates on the principle of **“contain through care.”**
Its upper levels emulate normalcy—gardens, books, conversation—while the lower levels remind all who serve that containment is mercy only when control is absolute.

Training fatalities, psychological erosion, and containment failures are accepted as statistical necessities.
The official doctrine defines loss as *“conversion of potential into data.”*
