#### Six Months Ago
---
As the helicopter decends below the clouds, an endless sea of ice comes into view. As far as the eye can see.
The snow fall here isn't soft or pleasant, it whips the side of the helicopter. The winds roar at a steady pace while the snow blows sideways.
As it touches down onto a faintly lit pad, the engines roar as its rotors tear through the thick snowfall.
As the engines slow to a crawl and the helicopters motor crackles as snow lands on it, the side door opens.
Four armed security officers rush to greet the visitors.
These officers are in white and blue camo to match the ice. Head-to-toe they have dyed wool incorporated into their load out to help keep them warm. Their weapons are heavy, large caliber carbines. They have a similar white/blue camo.
As the officers reach the helicopter they form a protective group, shielding the visitor as he exits.
Out from the helicopter comes a short, heavy-set, gentlemen. He's in his mid-50s. His jet-black suit is nicely buried under a thick, wool down coat. He has slick back silver hair and soft dark green eyes. His teeth are pristine white, like the snow all around him.
The gentleman made the brief fifty foot stroll from the helicopter to the massive looming building in front of him.
As he made his way to the door, two of the guards punched in a code by the door and swiped their security badges, as the gentleman made his way into the facilities first checkpoint room the armed guards vanished back into the snowy tundra.
As the large hydrologic door shut behind the gentleman and the room heated up, the large door adjacent to the one he enter opens.
On the other side of this door stands the [[Chief Officer of the Facility]], a few of his security detail, and the [[Chief Medical Officer]].
"Welcome to Site 73 Sir. Timerity Branches Maximum Security Enchanced-Individual Prison." Said the Chief Officer of the Facility.
"That's quite a mouthful." The Gentleman responded.
The Chief Medical Officer gestured with her hand to proceed down the left hall.
"We are on a tight schedule, if we could head this way to the recruits."
Quickly making their way across the facility and through a number of security checkpoints they reached Section B of the Site 73 complex.
Upon stepping through the doors they entered into a large room. This room spanned several football fields in length and width. It also vertically ascended and descended down by around five stories in both directions.
"Here is where we keep all of the Category 1-3 individuals who are taken in by CAIN.
They are detained here as less than prisoners under The Doctrine of Worms in 1720
Still, we do our best to treat them as humanely as possible." The Medical Officer said, her voice sharp and monotone. She continued,
"The individuals up to be recruited are on Cell Block 112 on the second lowest level. We can take the elevator down to see them now, we already have them in lineup."
"Perfect." Says the gentleman.
They proceed down the elevator, as they reach their floor they pan out and take their places amongst the open mesanine of the cell block.
As the Gentleman, the Chief Security Officer, and Medical Officer stand their, each shadowed closely by two armed guards, the prisoners are brought out in a line. Each tied to their neighbors by their necks, wrists, and feet. Bound in a way that one couldn't flee without another falling, bringing them down together. 
The gentleman in the suit is sitting at one of the tables, spread across the table are thick files, each tabbed with dozens—some with hundreds—of color-coded tabs. Stamped on the front of each folder is CLASSIFIED and CAIN and it's fist logo. Stamped along side it is the US Government seal, as well as a dozen other markings, coffee ring stains, and other signs that these documents have been poured over intricately for many months—or years—before they ended up where they are sitting now. 

"I don't think I need to explain to you why I'm here, but in case you've been living under a rock, let me made this clear as mud." The Chief Security Officer yells.
"You all have a Seed, a seed that grows in your head and that allows you to see the links between humanity, it also allows you special powers, for a cost. For this supernatural crime against the natural order of the world, you have been marked for death, your life was deemed null-and-void by the powers that be the second you manifested your Blasphemous powers. You're only saving grace is this man right here, you need not know his name, but he's from CAIN and he wants to hire you. He wants to take you from here, train you, and send you to fight SINS. That's what you things turn into when you use your powers too much or hurt people for your own selfish gain."

[[The Gentleman]] interrupts,
"That's enough. Let me be clear. You are a valuable asset to me. I want to take you from... this, (he gestures to the prison) and give you a more normal life. In return, I am tasking you with eradicating SINS. As you know, normal people like myself cannot see these creatures, they kill indiscriminately and sow seeds of panic among the world, you're only purpose in life is to ERASE THE STAINS." 
as he says those words the seed in your head burns, it brings you all to your knees. There's something in you, something fired up by the idea of killing SINS, it compels you, you have to listen to it.
"It seems I have your attention" the Gentleman says.
"Just nod if you'd like and I can sweep you out of here." 
(You all nod)
"Perfect! Let's see if we can't get more... familiar with one another."
