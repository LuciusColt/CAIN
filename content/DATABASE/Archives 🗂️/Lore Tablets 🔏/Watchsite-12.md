### **CAIN FACILITY DOSSIER — WATCHSITE-12 ("THE MIRROR GARDEN")**
---
###### ***🜃 “As Above, So Below.”***
---
**Designation**: Watchsite-12
**Classification**: Tier-Ω Research and Containment Complex
**Location**: Queen Maud Land, Antarctic Interior (approx. 71°S, 11°E)
**Operational Oversight**: Directorate 03 – Cognitive & Behavioral Division
**Status**: Active, Restricted

##### **I. STRUCTURE AND LOCATION**
Watchsite-12 is embedded into the granite shelf beneath a glacial valley in the Antarctic mountain chain known as the **Wohlthat Massif**.
The upper structure is partially submerged in ancient ice, its translucent glass dome projecting above the frozen surface. From within, personnel can see the endless white plain, auroras, and the constant churn of snowstorms against the curved viewport.
The complex extends nine levels downward, with each layer serving a distinct operational purpose:

| **Level** | **Designation**             | **Purpose**                                                           |
| --------- | --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **L-1**   | Arrival & Administration    | Airlock, decontamination, transit bays.                               |
| **L-2**   | Residential Quarters        | Personnel housing, cafeteria, gymnasium, gardens.                     |
| **L-3**   | Training & Simulation Halls | Holographic combat arenas, tactical conditioning.                     |
| **L-4**   | Research Wing               | Cognitive resonance labs, artifact testing chambers.                  |
| **L-5**   | Psychological Division      | Therapy suites, observation chambers, interview rooms.                |
| **L-6**   | Containment Ring            | Medium-threat entity containment.                                     |
| **L-7**   | Deep Storage                | Artifact vaults and cryogenic archives.                               |
| **L-8**   | The Dungeons                | High-threat confinement. Temperatures kept subzero.                   |
| **L-9**   | Core Systems / Reactor      | Geothermal-fusion hybrid providing power and heat. Access restricted. |

##### **II. ENVIRONMENT AND ATMOSPHERE**
Although sealed under kilometers of ice, Watchsite-12 maintains a steady 21 °C interior climate. Its heart is an artificial biosphere called the Mirror Garden — a greenhouse atrium spanning the entire L-2 mezzanine.
Inside the Garden, living trees grow in hydroponic soil; shallow streams run through transparent channels. This space doubles as both psychological rehabilitation area and observation habitat — the air is subtly laced with mood-stabilizing pheromones.
Lighting cycles simulate natural day/night to counteract polar insomnia. At “night,” the ceiling glass dims to reveal the Antarctic sky.

##### **V. CULTURE AND PSYCHOLOGY**
Life inside Watchsite-12 is paradoxical: Residents describe it as *“a terrarium of life inside a tomb of ice.”*
* **Executioner Trainees** often form close friendships through shared isolation. Friendships aren't ideal, neither are rivalries, both are kept in check by enforced professionalism.
* **Researchers** exhibit signs of long-term sensory adaptation: pallor, circadian drift, obsession with cataloguing dreams.
* **Guards** seldom speak; rumor says they rotate out every 60 days to prevent emotional freeze.
Entertainment consists of chess, cards, music, archived films, and the communal practice of watching the aurora when the storms clear. The facility holds unofficial traditions — trading handmade trinkets, leaving folded paper cranes in the greenhouse pond “for luck.”

##### **VI. SECURITY AND SECRETS**
* Every corridor has a mirrored observation panel. The official explanation: *“To study reflective response in isolation.”* Unofficially, they are believed to allow command to monitor emotional states.
* At least one containment unit beneath L-8 is off the books. Engineers hear heartbeats through the floor when power surges. This phenomenon is called the Tell-tale Heart of Watchsite-12.
* The Archivist occasionally sends personalized system messages referencing events no one recalls.
* Unauthorized areas are rumored to host cryo-preserved anomalies from defunct Watchsites.

##### **VII. NOTES ON LIVING CONDITIONS**
Despite its location, the base is comfortable:
* Bedrooms are warm, furnished simply with dull polymer furniture and a personal viewport to the ice.
* Food grown hydroponically in the Garden tastes “too clean.”
* The air hums faintly from geothermal vents.
