##### **I. DESCRIPTION**
Artifact 12-C is a pair of child’s safety scissors measuring approximately 13.2 cm in total length.
Handles composed of hardened cobalt-blue plastic; blades are standard stainless steel with rounded edges.
Despite mundane construction, artifact exhibits latent Anomaly resonance traced to the CAT 4 entity █████████ neutralized during Operation ██/██/20██.
> See Incident Report — [[Case File S-1420]] for further information.

The artifact’s anomalous nature manifests when within five meters of Host Candidate L.
Readings confirm low-frequency vibrational output (17.3 Hz) (55 dB) consistent with dormant Anomaly containment.
Energy signatures fluctuate during host sleep cycles and with emotional agitation.

##### **II. BEHAVIORAL CHARACTERISTICS**
While dormant, Artifact 12-C displays mild temperature variation (2–4°C above ambient) and faint sub-audible hum detectable only via specialized recording equipment.
The hum modulates into rhythmic pulses when Lucian exhibits elevated stress indicators (heart rate >110 bpm).
When exposed to reflective surfaces, the artifact produces visual misalignment phenomena, appearing in reflection 0.25 seconds delayed and sometimes rotated 180°.
During extended observation (>6 hrs), mirrored images of the artifact have been noted to move independently of both the physical object and host.

##### **III. ACTIVATION PARAMETERS**
Upon voluntary Burst activation by Host Candidate L, the artifact undergoes spontaneous transformation into dual short blades approximately 43 cm (17 in) in length, retaining metallic coloration but exhibiting semi-organic fractal growth patterns along the cutting edges.
Energy analysis indicates the blades are composed of the same molecular structure as the original scissors but folded through multiple microdimensional planes.
**Physical changes in host during activation include**:
- Rapid hair growth (approx. +4–6 inches)
- Ocular color shift to cobalt blue
- Swirling of pupil and iris
- Voice bifurcation (secondary tone present at ~300 Hz)
- Heightened aggression and psychomotor speed
- Progressive dissociation and partial ████████████ reemergence
- Sustained use beyond ██ minutes results in significant degradation of Host Cantidate L's mental health and stability.

Artifact remains bonded through subcutaneous tethering within host’s palm dermis. Forced removal attempts result in severe hemorhaging and mental strain.
