---
title: "[ DOOMED ]"
---
Objective:  
► Demonstrate your humanity  
► **Demonstrate your distance from humanity**  

Abilities:  
► **Xenoflesh**: Discard ‘1’s rolled for gaining strain.  
► **Humanity’s Last Breath**: Pick a stress mark you have. Evolve that stress mark. It no longer modifies resistance rolls.  
► **Quickening**: When you gain 2 or more stress as part of an action, your action also gains +1D.  
► **Sympathetic Mutation**: You may take 1d3 stress to grant an ally the benefits of any stress mark ability you have for one scene. Their body is infected by a lesser version of it that dissipates when the scene is over.  
► **Metamorphosis**: Between missions or when resting, you can gain 1 stress to re-roll the ability of one of your stress marks. If you roll an ability you already have, choose one instead.  

> Note: You may only pick this agenda if you have a stress mark. If it's your first agenda, you may start with a stress mark of your choice (still roll 1d6 for ability).
