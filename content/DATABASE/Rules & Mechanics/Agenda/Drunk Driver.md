---
title: "[ DRUNK DRIVER ]"
---
Objectives:  
► Let God take the wheel.  
► **Don’t hit anyone with your freaking car.**  

Abilities:
► **King’s Chariot**: Attacks made with your car can never be hard. It costs +3 scrip to replace when destroyed.
► **Turning Wheels I Laugh at Their Sobriety**: When you car takes damage, roll 1d6. On a 5-6 reduce that instance of damage to 1. 
► **Final Destination**: When you suffer instant death while driving your car you may crash it into anything dealing 2d3 slashes or 12 points of damage. 
► **Air Bags**: You take no damage from damage or harm involved with the destruction of your vehicle.
► **Backwards from 10**: You automatically succeed on rolls to retrieve your car keys from anyone or anywhere.

>God’s drunkest soldier:
>All interfacing rolls you make are 0D and ignore bonus die if you are sober. Your abilities gained from this agenda only activate when you are drunk.
