###### **MACHINE**
---

Objective:  
► Put the work before your own needs  
► **Take a break**  
► **Leave nobody behind** 

Abilities:  
► **The Work**: You can choose to gain +1D on any roll. If you do, take 1 damage for each time you’ve used this ability this mission (including this one). You can’t use this ability for the remainder of the hunt if you rest.  
► **Castle**: When any ally takes an injury, you erase 1 damage and gain +1D on your next action.
